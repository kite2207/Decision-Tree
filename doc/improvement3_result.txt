ANALYSIS
======================================================================

1. Gini Impurity:
   - Measures how often a randomly chosen element would be
     incorrectly classified.
   - Computationally faster (no log computation).
   - Tends to produce slightly larger/deeper trees.

2. Entropy (Information Gain):
   - Measures information gained by a split.
   - Slightly slower due to log computation.
   - May produce more balanced splits on some datasets.

3. Conclusion:
   Entropy outperforms Gini on this dataset.
   Accuracy gain: 0.57%, F1 gain: 1.77%.
   The information gain criterion finds better splits
   for this imbalanced classification problem.
